### Personal Development Plan

Through this internship, I have identified the need to deepen my technical knowledge and strengthen my ability to apply it in practical situations. Building on my academic background in cybersecurity and my experience deploying systems on AWS, I aim to pursue a career in cloud infrastructure, with a focus on security and system operations. To work toward this goal, I have set the following priorities:

- **Strengthen technical foundations:** Deepen my understanding of Linux administration, computer networking, and AWS service architecture, while practicing system design that incorporates appropriate access controls, monitoring, and data backup procedures. In each lab, I will focus on understanding the reasoning behind configuration choices and assessing their impact on security, availability, and operating costs.

- **Improve incident analysis and troubleshooting:** Develop a systematic troubleshooting process, from identifying symptoms and collecting logs and monitoring metrics to testing hypotheses, determining root causes, and verifying the effectiveness of fixes. I will create failure scenarios in a practice environment and document the resolution steps to build practical experience and identify measures to prevent recurring issues.

- **Develop technical communication and presentation skills:** Practice explaining technical topics with a clear structure covering the context, proposed approach, rationale, and results. Alongside improving technical reports and using architecture diagrams to illustrate ideas, I will actively participate in discussions, seek feedback, and strengthen my technical English to better understand documentation and communicate effectively.

- **Maintain continuous learning and professional work habits:** Create a phased learning plan with specific goals and deliverables, such as a working deployment, a technical guide, or an incident analysis report. I will regularly review my progress, adjust the plan based on feedback, and maintain consistent habits in documentation, time management, and accountability for the quality of my work.
