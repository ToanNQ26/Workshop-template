Having participated in the **AWS Workforce – First Cloud AI Journey** internship program, I would like to share my reflections and feedback. This opportunity helped me strengthen my knowledge of cloud computing, develop practical skills, and gain a clearer sense of my future career direction.

### Overall Assessment of the Program

**1. Content and Learning Path**  
The program provides a clear learning path, progressing from foundational concepts to advanced AWS services. The weekly structure helped me track my progress, manage my study time, and complete the planned learning objectives.

**2. Knowledge and Skills Gained**  
During the internship, I explored and practiced using services such as **IAM, Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, Amazon VPC, AWS Lambda, Amazon CloudWatch, Elastic Load Balancing, Amazon ECS, Amazon RDS, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon ElastiCache**. These activities helped me better understand how to select, configure, and combine AWS services to build a reliable, secure, and scalable system.

**3. Application to a Practical Project**  
What I valued most was the opportunity to apply what I had learned to my comic-reading website project. I deployed the backend on **Amazon EC2**, used **Nginx** as a reverse proxy and **PM2** to manage Node.js processes, configured **Security Groups** and an **Elastic IP**, and used an **Application Load Balancer** to distribute requests across two EC2 instances.

This process gave me a clearer understanding of the differences between running an application locally and deploying a working system in the cloud.

**4. Support from Mentors and the Organizing Team**  
The mentors and organizing team provided learning materials, guidance, and support throughout the internship. Their feedback encouraged me to investigate issues independently, review configurations, analyze logs, and gradually improve my troubleshooting skills.

**5. Personal Skills Developed**  
In addition to technical knowledge, the program helped me improve my ability to learn independently, manage time, find relevant documentation, write technical reports, and explain deployment procedures clearly and systematically.

---

### Survey Responses

- **What did you find most satisfying during the internship?**  
  What I found most satisfying was gaining hands-on experience with AWS and applying my knowledge to a working project. This made concepts such as servers, networking, security, monitoring, and load balancing more tangible and easier to understand.

- **What could the program improve for future cohorts?**  
  The program could include short group discussions at the end of each stage so that interns can share their progress, common issues, and troubleshooting approaches. More detailed guidance on AWS cost management and resource usage would also help students avoid unexpected charges.

- **Would you recommend the program to other students?**  
  Yes. I believe this program is well suited to students who want to learn about cloud computing, DevOps, and deploying a practical application on AWS.

---

### Suggestions and Aspirations

- **Suggestions:** Offer more guided sessions on practical system architecture, cost optimization, account security, and troubleshooting application deployments on AWS.

- **Aspirations:** I hope to continue participating in workshops and knowledge-sharing activities within the AWS Study Group community to keep my knowledge up to date, strengthen my technical skills, and connect with others who share similar career interests.

Finally, I would like to thank the mentors, organizing team, and **AWS Study Group** community for their support and for enabling me to complete the internship program. The knowledge and experience I gained will provide an important foundation for my future development in **Backend Development and Cloud Computing**.
