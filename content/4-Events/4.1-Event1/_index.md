## Reflection on "AI Revolution & Open Claw"

### General Event Information

**Event Name**: AWS VIETNAM COMMUNITY MEETUP AI REVOLUTION & OPEN CLAW  
**Date**: July 25, 2026  
**Location**: 7th Floor, Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cat Linh, Dong Da, Hanoi.  
**Organizer**: AWS VIETNAM COMMUNITY First Cloud Journey team  
**Role**: Attendee

### Event Objectives
- Update knowledge of the latest AI trends on AWS.
- Introduce AI Agent and open-source AI applications.
- Share how to choose suitable AI Agent patterns.
- Explore how to turn AI into practical business value.
- Connect and exchange experiences with the AWS Vietnam community.

### Speakers
- Henry Duc Bui - Head of Engineering at CloudThinker
  - Topic: Ship Fast with AI, Not by AI
- Nguyen Thu (Yuna) - AI Solutions and Business Specialist
  - Topic: From AI Trends to Business Value - Sales Track
- Nam La - Cloud Engineer at Cloudino / AWS Vietnam User Group Admin
  - Topic: AI Trends Through the Lens of an Infrastructure Engineer
- Tuan Vu - AWS Community Builder
  - Topic: OpenClaw - The Rise and Practice of Open-Source AI Agents

### Key Highlights

- **Current situation and limitations:** GenAI and coding assistance tools significantly increase coding speed in the Inner Loop. However, bringing a product into production in the Outer Loop can still be limited by evaluation, testing, integration, and deployment steps.
- **Solutions for the Outer Loop:** The focus is not only on building more powerful AI models, but also on automating processes through lint rules, CI checks, skills, and documentation in the repository.
- **Infrastructure trends:** Cloud infrastructure management is gradually shifting from a reactive to a proactive approach through AIOps, FinOps, and proactive security. The future is moving toward infrastructure that can automatically manage and optimize itself with the support of AI Agents.
- **Practical AI applications:** AI is gradually becoming a support tool for many activities, including software development, system operations, data analysis, and infrastructure optimization.

### What I Learned

- **Changing how AI-generated code is evaluated:** Focus on design structure, how components communicate, and risks related to state, error handling, and security instead of only checking individual lines of code.
- **Automating and preserving knowledge:** Turn rules and common errors into documentation, lint rules, or skills that can be reused across different sessions.
- **Strengthening testing:** Use AI to help create test cases and testing tools for critical functions, thereby improving system reliability.
- **The changing role of engineers:** Engineers are increasingly shifting from directly operating systems to designing, controlling, and monitoring systems supported by AI.

### Applying the Knowledge to My Studies

- **Organizing documentation in a repository:** Learn how to build documentation and project rules directly in a repository to support both development and the use of AI Agents.
- **Understanding the Inner Loop and Outer Loop:** Clarify how AI can support the process from coding and testing to software integration and deployment.
- **Learning about AIOps and FinOps:** Explore how AI can be used to detect anomalies, monitor systems, and optimize costs when operating in the Cloud.
- **Developing AI collaboration skills:** Practice prompt engineering and context engineering, and learn about control mechanisms such as guardrails and rollback.
- **Connecting with AWS knowledge:** Broaden my perspective on cloud infrastructure, monitoring, cost optimization, and security through the practical content shared at the event.

### Event Experience

- **A practical perspective:** Approach AI from the infrastructure and software development perspectives instead of focusing only on AI models or data science.
- **A clearer understanding of AI's role:** Recognize that AI should be used as a tool to support and extend engineers' capabilities, rather than completely replacing human thinking and judgment.
- **Exposure to new technology trends:** Become familiar with concepts such as Agentic AI, AIOps, FinOps, and the trend toward building automated infrastructure.
- **Learning direction:** Gain a practical perspective for studying Cloud, Infrastructure, Software Development, and AI, while identifying the skills that need further development.
- **Memorable message:** *"Ship fast with AI, not by it"* emphasizes using AI as a tool to increase productivity, improve quality, and support system development.

### Lessons Learned

- **AI is a support tool, not a replacement for critical thinking:** Effective use of AI must be accompanied by human verification, evaluation, and decision-making.
- **Focus on quality, not just speed:** Faster code generation does not necessarily mean faster product completion. Testing, integration, deployment, and system quality must also be prioritized.
- **Prioritize automating repetitive tasks:** Rules, common errors, and review processes should be automated through lint rules, CI/CD, and documentation.
- **Strengthen cloud infrastructure knowledge:** Continue learning more deeply about monitoring, security, cost optimization, and automation to build a strong foundation for operating Cloud systems.
- **Proactively learn about Agentic AI:** As AI Agents become more involved in system development and operations, it is important to know how to build appropriate context, prompts, and control mechanisms.
- **Develop system design and monitoring skills:** In addition to hands-on skills, focus on analyzing architecture, identifying risks, and controlling system operations.
- **Combine theory with practice:** The topics shared at the event helped connect knowledge of AWS, Cloud, and Software Development with technology trends being applied in practice.
